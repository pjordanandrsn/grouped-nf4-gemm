What the matrix says that the headline doesn’t

bf16Unsloth’s bf16-resident column is almost entirely theirs: 2.6–5.3× faster at prefill on the H100. gnf4’s advantage is the 4-bit-storage regime specifically — where a bf16 GEMM must first materialize the weights — and is not a general claim.
lossTwo H100 cells go to Unsloth: OLMoE gate_up prefill (0.890) and gpt-oss-120b down decode_m8 (0.997). OLMoE gate_up is the smallest-expert census shape and a known loser class in this repo’s published history; it wins on the 4090, so the loss is SM-count-dependent.
missP1’s predicted band (2.0–6.0×) misses on the H100 at 1.70. The bar (≥1.0 on ≥7/8 cells) passes; the prediction does not. Amendment 1 registered that miss pre-data and it reproduced.
proxyThe proxy/real column is the correction this repo owed: tgale96’s grouped_gemm.ops.gmm — what every prior “unsloth” receipt actually ran — is 1.33× slower at median than Unsloth’s real kernel, up to 3.40×. The published 4.67× was measured against a weaker opponent. Superseded, not rescaled.
